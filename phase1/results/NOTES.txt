AI in Medicine (MD5001) - Bowel-sound detection project
Phase 1: classical vs deep baseline, leakage-controlled evaluation
Run: Kaggle notebook akhilanskmd24b001/notebook98cfb36f44, CPU, 584 min, finished 21 Oct 2026

DATA
Kaggle "Bowel sounds" (robertnowak/bowel-sounds; Ficek et al., Sensors 2021, doi:10.3390/s21227602; CC BY-NC 4.0)
1606 two-second clips (44.1 kHz), 427 recording prefixes, 6413 expert-annotated bowel-sound events.
Task: detect bowel sound in each 10 ms frame (positive frame rate 9.3%), plus event-level matching.

FEATURES / MODELS
64-band log-mel spectrogram at 8 kHz, 10 ms hop.
LightGBM: per-frame features (mel, delta, +/-50 ms context mean/std), class-weighted.
CRNN: 4 conv blocks + bidirectional GRU, frame-wise output, SpecAugment-lite, 30 epochs.
Decision threshold tuned on an inner validation split of TRAINING data only.

EVALUATION PROTOCOLS
official : authors' train/test split from files.csv
grouped  : 5-fold GroupKFold by recording prefix (N in N_x), so no recording is in both train and test
2 random seeds each.

KEY RESULTS (mean +/- SD)
                         Frame F1        Event F1        Event recall   AUROC
Official  LightGBM       0.778 +/- 0.005 0.696 +/- 0.002 0.639          0.976
Official  CRNN           0.793 +/- 0.003 0.714 +/- 0.000 0.619          0.989
Grouped   LightGBM       0.745 +/- 0.017 0.680 +/- 0.026 0.632          0.967
Grouped   CRNN           0.805 +/- 0.026 0.703 +/- 0.023 0.621          0.985

LEAKAGE FINDING
In the authors' split, 282 of 321 test clips (88%) come from recordings that also appear in training
(183 of 208 test recordings). Under leak-free grouped evaluation LightGBM drops (frame F1 0.778 -> 0.745)
while the CRNN holds (~0.80), so the CRNN generalises better across recordings.

LIMITATIONS
- Dataset has no subject IDs; "grouped by recording prefix" is the strictest split possible until the
  authors share a file-to-subject mapping (requested by email 21 Oct 2026).
- Recall is the weak point: ~38% of bowel sounds missed at the chosen thresholds.
